# YvyShift · Demo video script (30 seconds or less)

Prepared on 15 Nov 2026 to replace `PITCH_SCRIPT.md` as the demo. The official Project Submission Guide says the demo must be a video of 30 seconds or less (or at most 7 slides), and that "Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging". This script runs about 27 seconds at a calm pace, which leaves 3 seconds of margin.

Everything said here is visible in the app or documented in `docs/VALIDACION_EXTERNA.md` and `docs/auditoria_competitiva/03_SCIENTIFIC_AND_TECHNICAL_AUDIT.md`. Do not add numbers that are not on this page.

## The core message (the one sentence judges should remember)

> NASA data show what each extra crop costs in water. We checked it against 23 real harvests. The farmer decides what that water is worth.

## Why each line is there

The first two lines cover what the official structure asks for in the first ten seconds: who the team is, which challenge, and the motivation. The 2022 drought figure comes from FAO (production fell from 10.5 to 5.2 million tonnes), as documented in `VALIDACION_EXTERNA.md`.

The "Show NASA data only" shot is the one thing no other team will show. It answers the audit's finding N-1 head-on: with NASA climate data alone, soybean plus winter fallow wins most seasons, and what flips the ranking is how much the farmer values soil cover and a second harvest. Saying it openly turns the main weakness into the headline.

The validation line speaks directly to the Validity criterion and to the NASA Earth Science judge on the Asunción panel.

## Recording checklist

1. Use the published URL, not a local file, so the address bar shows the real link.
2. Browser zoom at 110 to 125 % so numbers read on a phone. Hide bookmarks and other tabs.
3. Light theme (the v4 forest green, cream and wheat palette). Language: English.
4. Start the screen recording on the Home view with Itapúa selected and the 2021–22 scenario.
5. Record the narration separately and lay it over the screen capture; it is easier to stay under 30 seconds.
6. Burn in English subtitles even if the voice is in English: many judges watch without sound.
7. Export, then check the real duration in the file properties. **It must be 30 seconds or less.** If it is 31, cut the end card to 2 seconds.
8. Upload as public or unlisted (YouTube or Google Drive with "anyone with the link"). Open it in a private window to confirm it plays without signing in.

## Rules to respect in the video

Do not say "recommends", "predicts", "your field's data" or "AI". The app compares and explores, uses a regional NASA POWER cell of about 56 × 62 km, and does not use AI at runtime. Do not use NASA logos or any NASA branding. If any AI-generated image or video is used (none is needed), it must carry a visible watermark. Real screen captures of the app do not need one.

## Plan B: 7 slides

If the video cannot be recorded in time, the same story fits in 7 English slides: 1) title and team, 2) the 2022 drought, 3) how it works (NASA POWER, FAO-56 water balance), 4) the NASA lens and N-1, 5) validation against 23 harvests, 6) limits and what a local agronomist still needs to check, 7) link to the app. Use real screenshots.
