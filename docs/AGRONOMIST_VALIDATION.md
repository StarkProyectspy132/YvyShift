# Validation form for an agronomist or farmer

Time: 20 minutes. A call, a WhatsApp voice note or a message is enough.
This is the step that most improves the project: it checks the crop calendars and thresholds, which are still assumptions.

**Before starting:** ask for permission to quote their name and role. If they prefer not, write only "agronomist from Itapúa" or similar. Write down what they say as said; if they do not know something, write "does not know". Do not fill anything in from memory.

## 3. Usefulness

Show them the app on the phone for two minutes (Itapúa, 2021–22 season) and ask:

1. Would this be useful to you, or to a farmer? For what?
2. What is missing for you to use it?
3. What do you think is wrong?

## What to do with the answers

With this form completed, in less than an hour the team can:
- Correct the calendars and thresholds in the engine and recompute everything.
- Change "assumption" to "validated with [name]" where it applies.
- Put the quote and the figure on the project page and in the presentation.
- Enable exporting the calendar to the phone's agenda, which is on hold until the calendars are validated.
